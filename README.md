# International Community School of Abidjan (ICSA)

Welcome to the official GitHub organization for the International Community School of Abidjan.

## About ICSA

The International Community School of Abidjan (ICSA) is an American international school providing quality education to students from diverse backgrounds in Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire. We are committed to fostering academic excellence, global citizenship, and innovative thinking through technology and collaborative learning.

🌐 **Website**: [www.icsabidjan.org](https://www.icsabidjan.org/)  
📍 **Location**: Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire  
🎓 **Mission**: Empowering students through innovative education and technology

## Our GitHub Community

This organization hosts educational projects, learning resources, and collaborative initiatives developed by our school community including:

- Student coding projects and assignments
- Educational tools and resources
- School administration and management tools
- Open source educational initiatives
- Technology learning materials
